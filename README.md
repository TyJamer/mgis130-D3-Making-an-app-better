# mgis130-D3-Making-an-app-better
New App attempt

For my app, I want to make a budgeting app that allows its users, primarily college kids to find the smartest way for them to continue saving their money. I also want to include exchange rate API for international students who are used to using other currencies.  

Data Layer; This layer would consist of transactions, income, expenses, different types of budgeting, and user accounts. Check the amount of money you earned, whether it be salary based or weekly check based, and input it into the app. There will be an option to sign in but you can also continue as a guest. 
The Processing Layer; This layer would consist of the calculations, categorizations, budget tracking, and an analysis. Include different budgeting styles like the 50-30-20 rule, 60-20-20 rule, 70-20-10 rule, 80-20 rule, zero-based budgeting, and values-based budgeting. Include the categorizations of wants, needs, expenses, debt, where you would like to insert your money. 
The presentation Layer; This layer would consist of dashboards, charts, and budget forms. It would give you different options on ways to budget your money (different percentage rules). I want there to be a dropdown menu showing the different categorizations of wants, needs, expenses, debt, and income, so you can easily track what money goes where. When you first open the app, after the sign in page, show the different budget styles that the user can work with. I want the app to be a white background, with a somewhat fancy font, have the colors of the charts be black and orange.
